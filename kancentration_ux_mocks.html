Confirmed Design Decisions

1. "Confusable Twin Trap" Feedback (Default: ON, Settable) When a player flips two cards from the same confusable quad (e.g. 木 and 本, or 持 and 特), an amber callout highlights the structural difference. Enabled by default and toggleable in Settings.
2. Kana Readings by Default + Romaji Setting (Default: OFF) The Kanji Inspector displays clean Japanese Kana readings (ON: ジ • KUN: も.つ) by default, with a Settings switch to show Romaji transliterations.
3. In-Situ Typography Toggle (Mincho Serif vs Gothic Sans) Settings includes a live font style switch so players can compare and play with either traditional Mincho (Serif) or modern Gothic (Sans-Serif) kanji glyphs.
4. Discoverable Wiktionary Seal & Locked Viewport The Kanji Inspector features a tactile Hanko Seal Tile with an explicit Wiktionary ↗ badge, and fixed proportional slots prevent any board layout shift.
