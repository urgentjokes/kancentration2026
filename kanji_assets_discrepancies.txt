1. [grade_1.json & kanji_base_pack.json] grade1_quad_01_person_enter / quad_03_person_enter — 人 (kanji_hito_person) vs 入 (kanji_iru_enter)
   - Current distinctionTip for 人: "Left stroke is drawn first and stands taller; right stroke leans against it."
   - Current distinctionTip for 入: "Right stroke starts higher and sweeps over to the right; left stroke tucks underneath."
   - Discrepancy: MISLEADING STROKE-ORDER CLAIM. In 入, the left stroke (丿) is ALSO drawn first (Stroke 1 is the shorter left-sweeping stroke 丿, and Stroke 2 is the taller right-sweeping stroke ㇏). Stating "Left stroke is drawn first" as the distinction for 人 falsely implies that in 入 the right stroke is drawn first.

2. [grade_1.json] grade1_quad_04_king_jewel — 王 (kanji_ou_king)
   - Current distinctionTip: "Three equal horizontal bars joined by a central vertical stroke, with no dot."
   - Discrepancy: FACTUALLY INCORRECT PROPORTIONS. The three horizontal bars of 王 are NOT equal in length (the bottom bar is longest, the top bar is medium, and the middle bar is shortest).

3. [grade_1.json] grade1_quad_09_power_katakana_ka — カ (katakana_ka)
   - Current distinctionTip: "Katakana 'ka' (derived from 加); the left-sweeping stroke does NOT pierce through the top, meeting neatly at the top-left vertex."
   - Discrepancy: FACTUALLY INCORRECT. In Katakana カ, the left-sweeping stroke (ノ) DOES pierce upward through the top horizontal line, just like 力 (it is 刀 or ク where the left stroke does not pierce above the top horizontal bar, NOT カ). カ and 力 differ in overall scale/angularity, not in whether the vertical-left sweep pierces the top horizontal bar.

   - Current distinctionTip: "Kanji for 'round'; features an extra accent drop dot (丶) inside the upper-right bend of the hooked stroke."
   - Discrepancy: FACTUALLY INCORRECT POSITION. In 丸, the extra dot stroke (丶) is placed on the LEFT side (crossing the downward-left sweep 丿), NOT "inside the upper-right bend of the hooked stroke".

5. [grade_1.json] grade1_quad_16_evening_katakana_ta — タ (katakana_ta)
   - Current distinctionTip: "Katakana 'ta' (derived from 多); the crossing horizontal-diagonal stroke breaks distinctly through the left slant."
   - Discrepancy: FACTUALLY INCORRECT. In Katakana タ, the interior diagonal tick (3rd stroke) starts at the left slant and goes down-right toward the right-hand curved sweep; it does NOT break through the left slant.

In 矢 (5 strokes), the central vertical/sweeping stroke (丿, stroke 4) does NOT pierce above the top horizontal bar either (that is 夫 vs 天 in grade4_quad_04_husband_heaven!). Instead, 矢 begins with a short downward-left diagonal tick (ノ, stroke 1) at the LEFT end of the top horizontal bar (forming 𠂉), and the top horizontal bar starts from the middle of that left slant rather than being pierced through by a vertical/slanted stroke.

A. Wrong, Confusable-Trap, or Archaic Meanings in JSON
------------------------------------------------------
1. [grade_4.json & kanji_base_pack.json] grade4_quad_01_notyet_end / quad_05_end_notyet — 末 (kanji_sue_end)
   - JSON meanings: ["end", "tip", "future"] (and ["end", "tip", "extremity", "future"] in kanji_base_pack.json)
   - Grade_4.csv Meaning: "end"
   - Issue: CONFUSABLE MEANING TRAP WITH 未. In Japanese, "future" (未来 mirai) is written with 未 ("not yet"), NOT 末 ("end")! Listing "future" as a primary meaning of 末 (derived from the secondary compound 行く末 yukusue "one's future fate") in the very quad contrasting 未 and 末 actively misleads learners into associating 末 with "future".

2. [grade_6.json] grade6_quad_17_imperial_steps_stairs — 陛 (kanji_hei_imperial_steps)
   - JSON meanings: ["highness", "imperial palace steps"]
   - Grade_6.csv Meaning: "majesty"
   - Issue: WRONG ROYAL TITLE ("highness" vs "majesty"). In Japanese, 陛 (in 陛下 Heika) means "Majesty" (for an Emperor, Empress, King, or Queen), whereas "Highness" (for a Prince or Princess) is 殿 (in 殿下 Denka).

3. [grade_6.json] grade6_quad_18_pure_settle — 納 (kanji_osameru_settle)
   - JSON meanings: ["settlement", "obtain", "reap", "pay"]
   - Grade_6.csv Meaning: "settlement"
   - Issue: CONFUSED WITH 収 (osameru). 納 means "pay (taxes/fees), supply, deliver, store, accept/settle". It does NOT mean "obtain" or "reap" — those are the meanings of its homophone 収 (as in 収穫 "harvest/reap" and 収得 "obtain", see Grade_6.csv line 25: "859,収,obtain,shū,osa-meru,4").

   - JSON meanings: ["ice", "hail"]

5. [grade_5.json] grade5_quad_14_burn_sort — 然 (kanji_shikari_sort)

   - Issue: Leading with "sort of thing" (and naming the quad "grade5_quad_14_burn_sort" and id "kanji_shikari_sort") comes from an obscure KANJIDIC suffix gloss. In standard Japanese, 然 means "so", "thus", "in that way", "natural (自然)", or "however/although".

   - JSON meanings: ["discern", "know", "write"]
   - Grade_5.csv Meaning: "discriminating"
   - Issue: "write" is an archaic Classical Chinese meaning of 識 (to record/inscribe), not a modern Japanese meaning. Standard meanings are "knowledge", "awareness", "discern", "discriminate".
