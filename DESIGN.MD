# Kancentration: A kanjified twist on the traditional card-matching game of Concentration.

## Game description:

### Gameplay

* Turn Sequence: Players take turns in clockwise order. On a turn, the active player flips over any two cards of their choice, one at a time, making sure all players can see their faces.

** Matching

If the cards match, the cards are left facing up (revealed), and the player scores a point. The game will have a setting that determines whether scoring means that the player gets another turn, or his turn ends even when he scores.

If the cards do not match: Both cards are turned face down again in the exact same positions. The player then repeats the process. (Or, in two-player mode, the turn passes to the other player.)

** Tracking

All players observe flipped cards during every turn to memorize their locations for future rounds. Over time, they memorize what cards are where, and they become more successful in their ability to find matches. The player with the most matches (points) wins.

Kancentration's interesting twist is that there are pairs of kanji pairs in which each pair is subtly different from the other pair. For example, 日-日 vs 曰-曰 , or 持-持 vs 特-特.

Game boards of 4 x 4, 6 x 6, and 8 x 8

Under the game board is a display of the currently tapped kanji and its definition. If you tap that display of the kanji, you're taken to its page on Wiktionary. For example, if 曰 is displayed, and you tape it, you get taken to https://en.wiktionary.org/wiki/%E6%9B%B0.

** Other characteristics

The game should come with 16 pairs of pairs. More can be downloaded as purchasable DLC.

The game tracks time and score, and has a 1-player and 2-player mode. It also has a time-attack mode, which can be 1, 3, or 5 minutes.
